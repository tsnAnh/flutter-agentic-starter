# Current Preferences

* Keep reports concise.
* Sacrifice grammar for concision in reports when useful.
* Put unresolved questions at the end of reports.
* Prefer minimal, focused edits and direct status updates.
* Use boring, obvious solutions before adding abstractions or dependencies.
